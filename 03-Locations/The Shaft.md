## Description
The giant room with the deep hole, reached down the hole in the
[[Inverted Pyramid]] in [[Session 02 - 2026-08-29]]: a handle linked by one
great leather band to a 40 ft metal wheel, and a plain metal chain running down
the hole past every level of [[The Cradle]].

## Session Log

[[Vicaster]] dropped a stone lit with *light* down the hole. It shrank out of sight and never landed.
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: The [[Shaft Giant]] climbed the chain, filling the shaft. The party crossed the gap level by level to stay ahead of it.
