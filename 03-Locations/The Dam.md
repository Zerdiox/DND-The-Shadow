## Description
A structure containing 6 sarcophagi holding the remains of 6 Lemurian engineers. Features a room with 13 iron pots sealed with magic, and a tomb with a ship sarcophagus.

The party reached it by boat, ferried by [[Rem Koolhuis]], shortly before the events of [[Session 01 - 2026-08-01]].
