## Description
The party's home town, and the first place in the vault that is theirs rather
than somewhere they are exploring.

It was invaded by dwarves at some point in the earlier unrecorded stretch of the
campaign, and [[The Grove]] helped drive them out. By the time the party
returned in [[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]] the city had
flourished: trees blooming, fields worked, the place doing well in general.

[[Theodin]] leads it. [[The Grove]] is here, and has been working on the plague
for a long time — which makes Orebridge the lead [[Aurorion]] gave
[[Vicaster]] for [[The Shadow]].
