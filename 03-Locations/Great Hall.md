## Description
An enormous empty hall at the far end of [[The Bridge]]. It holds two bronze
cylinders, each with a door and a stair leading down inside it, and a ladder
running both up and down. The ladder up leads to a shelf in the cave wall and on
to the [[Armoury]].

## Session Log
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: Reached across the bridge. The party took the ladder up; the cylinders and the downward routes are unexplored.
- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: The party took the downward ladder, which passes [[The Puddle]] on its way to the [[Astral Chamber]].
- [[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]]: Both cylinders explored at last, and they turned out to be one loop rather than two ways down — the left one holds the caged stair through the [[Butterfly Cave]] to the [[Glass Floor Room]], whose far stair comes back up inside the right one.
