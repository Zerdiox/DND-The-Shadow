## Description
A gallery of perfectly polished blocks on platforms, each a labelled
cross-section of strata:

- **Vampire strata** — white-yellow, full of bones, teeth and other human remains
- **Sword strata** — rusted metal packed with daggers, swords and other blades,
  standing for a stratum a kilometre tall
- **Strata of fractured minds** — cast in resin; layers of muscle and fat
- **Strata of the Gackling moon** — bands of green, orange, ochre and yellow,
  with the exoskeletons of crawling things between them
- **Carbon layers of the burned city** — a block that is a piece of building:
  brick, window and downpipe
