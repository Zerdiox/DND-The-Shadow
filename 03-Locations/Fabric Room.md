## Description
A room below the [[Cervit Storage]], heaped with piles of fabric. Ladders lead
further down, to the [[Poison Cage]] and the [[Strata Gallery]].

## Session Log
- [[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]: Reached by [[Drivik]] sliding down the chain; the others climbed down after him. On the way back, spectres wrapped in chains rose out of the fabric. The party won quickly, but the spectres escaped once beaten.
