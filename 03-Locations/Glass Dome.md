## Description
A round, ball-like chamber reached through the portal in the [[Glass Statue Room]], with a single door at the far end. Beyond the door is a tube-like
passage of segments that narrow progressively, too small for two people abreast.

- [[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]: [[Zeernebooch]] revealed itself here and spoke with the party, then sealed the door and declared it was hungry. The party escaped.
