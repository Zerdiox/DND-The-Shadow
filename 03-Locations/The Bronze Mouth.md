## Description
Up a long staircase from the [[Armoury]]: a small room with a long hall leading
off it. The hall ends in nothing — until you look up. A shaft runs 30 to 40 feet
up through the ceiling to a bronze mouth, which is a trap door.

It opens into the [[Inverted Pyramid]], among the statues. This is the way out
of [[The Cradle]].
