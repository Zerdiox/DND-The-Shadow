## Description
A room holding a metal cage with a glowing green ball inside — a portal to the
para-elemental plane of poison. The cage cannot be crossed, and getting close is
harmful.

## Session Log
- [[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]: [[Vicaster]]'s familiar, in sphinx of wonder form, died painfully passing through the cage; he resummoned it and gave it candy. Getting close cost Vicaster 22 poison damage. [[Drivik]] shot an arrow into the ball and it vanished inside. Vicaster's arcana identified the plane, and the party passed through keeping clear of the cage.
- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: The alchemy hall's books suggest this portal and the astral one were part of the same work — see [[The Portals]].
