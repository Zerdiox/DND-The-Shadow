## Description
What is inside the left-hand bronze cylinder of the [[Great Hall]]. The stair
goes down through rock and then out into an open cave, and for that stretch it
runs inside a metal cage.

Black butterflies drift in the open cave. They look like holes in the air.

The stair comes out at the [[Glass Floor Room]].
