## Description
A room below the [[Crystal Hall]] filled with shells — clams, spirals, crab
shells and dried coral, all of them empty. It reads like a stretch of sea floor.
No ladder leads further down from here; a tunnel on the far side of the shaft
leads to the [[Art Store]].

## Session Log
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: [[Muradin]] went in first. The party found the shells empty and no way down, and crossed the shaft to the art store.
