## Description
A round chamber whose walls carry hieroglyphs and other drawings. In the middle
stands something like a spider curled up on its back, and held in its legs is a
great block of ice encasing a human female form — [[The Woman in the Ice]].

## Session Log
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: [[Vicaster]], [[Aslaug]] and [[Echo of Twilight]] came down here first. The [[Shaft Giant]] reached an arm down the ladder and seized Vicaster, and dropped him when Aslaug cast *command*. Vicaster then broke the ice block, releasing the wraith, and the party took [[The Black Book]] from the body.
