## Description
A large room of tables, desks and alcoves, all of it alchemical equipment,
dried out and gone gooey. Bookcases and cupboards line the walls: one cupboard
of logbooks and diaries, another of nineteen unlabelled vials, and shelves of
large leather-bound project books with code names like *Angelic Pig*.

The books cover planar travel, magic for military fabrication, cursing fabric
and clothes, three on poisons and two on diseases. Nothing on undead. One
records how the builders opened a way into the Astral Plane using the blood of
[[The Astral Being]].

A giant metal door stands on the far side, closing off [[The Sealed Room]].
