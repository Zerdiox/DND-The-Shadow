## Description
A square room 30 by 50 feet at the foot of the ladder from the [[Great Hall]].
[[The Astral Being]] hangs from the ceiling on chains in the middle of it,
dripping white blood into flasks that overflowed long ago. A table holds the
clay construction the party repaired into the [[Clay Mapmaker]]. A door leads to
a stairway down a giant square hole.

## Session Log
- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: [[Aslaug]] recognised the thing on the chains. [[Vicaster]] mended the smashed clay construction and kept it. [[Muradin]] opened the door with *guidance* and the party went down.
