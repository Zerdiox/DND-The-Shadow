## Description
A room behind a giant metal door off the [[Alchemy Hall]], sealed and
pressurised. Opening it vents a cloud of poisonous air — metallic, acidic,
foul — and leaves an oily sheen on the skin.

## Session Log
- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: [[Drivik]] knocked on the door and something knocked back. [[Muradin]] opened it, releasing the gas and [[The Enlarged Microbe]]. The party killed it after a hard fight, then took a long rest.
