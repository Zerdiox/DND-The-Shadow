## Description
At the foot of the stair through the [[Butterfly Cave]]. The floor is glass,
and it looks down into the [[Poison Cage]] — the room holding the portal to the
para-elemental plane of poison, which the party had walked through back in
[[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]].

A staircase at the far end goes back up, and comes out in the other bronze
cylinder of the [[Great Hall]]. So the two cylinders are one loop, not two
routes down.
