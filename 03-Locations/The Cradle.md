## Description
The facility beneath the [[Tower]], entered down the hole in the
[[Inverted Pyramid]], and the goal of the party's explorations. Everything the
party has explored below the pyramid lies inside it — the room of
[[The Shaft]], the [[Throne Room]], the glass rooms, the [[Library]] and
[[Vault]], and the levels further down the shaft.

According to the [[Crystal Sisters]], it was built to investigate deadly and
dangerous things. Among them, as the [[Disease Books]] in the [[Alchemy Hall]]
show, both halves of [[The Shadow]]. Its central point is an observatory at the very bottom, where
the builders searched for the materials their research needed.

[[Marius Caldus]] refused to say anything about the cradle when the party
questioned him in [[Session 01 - 2026-08-01]].
