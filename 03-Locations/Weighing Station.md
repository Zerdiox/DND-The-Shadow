## Description
A staircase running parallel to [[The Shaft]] descends to a platform hung on
chains — a weight system for ore, with empty tubs. The stairs end in a cave at
the bottom of the station, where an open door gives onto the shaft and its
chain. Across the gap is the [[Cervit Storage]].
