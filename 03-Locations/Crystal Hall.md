## Description
A fine chamber made of crystal, about 20 ft below the [[Poison Cage]]. Spirals
are engraved into the floor, and the [[Crystal Sisters]] lounge on platforms of
salt.

## Session Log
- [[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]: [[Drivik]] scouted it and scrambled back; [[Vicaster]]'s familiar made first contact; the party came down, greeted the sisters and returned the stolen heart. Drivik licked a platform and confirmed it is salt.
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: The party jumped the shaft back into the salt chamber to get around the [[Shaft Giant]], and found it empty — the [[Crystal Sisters]] had returned to their own plane. Took the ladder down to the [[Shell Room]].
