## Description
A large hall above the [[Great Hall]], reached by a ladder to a shelf in the
cave wall and a second ladder up from there. It was a weapon store: the racks
stand empty, but ten iron chests remain against the wall.

## Session Log
- [[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]: [[Drivik]] found the chests locked but untrapped and picked all ten. Their contents ran from crossbow gear and climbing kit to the [[Heartseeker Dagger]], the [[Mud Grenades]] and the [[Red Lanterns]].
- [[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]]: Passed through on the way out, and up a long staircase beyond it to [[The Bronze Mouth]].
