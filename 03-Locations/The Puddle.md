## Description
A tunnel opening off the ladder halfway down from the [[Great Hall]], holding
a large puddle. The water is oily to the touch and very salty. Three things lie
under it: the [[Green Sword]], the [[Silver Tongue]], and
[[The Screaming Baby]].
