## Description
Sturdy metal tables beneath a broken machine hanging from the ceiling — a
half-dome of many panels. Against one wall stand four crystal orbs on pedestals,
each about a third larger than a football, holding mutated variants of
underground plants. Skin tingles near them. They are very heavy, detect magic
shows nothing, and they appear to be alchemical. The room overlooks
[[The Shaft]] and reveals another room below.
