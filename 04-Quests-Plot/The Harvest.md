## Summary
[[Rem Koolhuis]] is taking [[The Dam]].

The party came back up out of [[The Cradle]], walked to the dam, and found him
standing on top of it. He told them the plan himself: hold the dam, use the
melt from the coming winter to power the three golems now standing in front of
it, and harvest magical power with them.

He is the wizard who ferried the party to the dam in the first place, before
[[Session 01 - 2026-08-01]] — so whatever this is, it did not start this
session.

The party did not fight him, did not agree to anything, and walked on to
[[Orebridge]]. What he intends to do with the power, and what it costs anyone
else, is not established. Neither is whether the dam's six Lemurian engineers
or the tomb below matter to him.
