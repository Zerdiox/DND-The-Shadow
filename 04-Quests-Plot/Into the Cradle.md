## Summary
The party came to the Cradle to find a way to stop [[The Shadow]]. This note
tracks the descent itself; the plague is tracked in that one.

The Cradle has been the goal of the party's explorations — [[Marius Caldus]]
wouldn't speak of it in [[Session 01 - 2026-08-01]]. The [[Crystal Sisters]]
confirmed the party has reached it: the complex they have been exploring beneath
the [[Inverted Pyramid]] is the Cradle, a facility built to investigate deadly and
dangerous things.

Next objective: the observatory at the very bottom, the Cradle's central point,
where the builders searched for the materials their research needed.

**The party have left the Cradle without reaching it.** In
[[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]] they finished the
[[Great Hall]]'s two bronze cylinders — which proved to be one loop, not two
ways down — and then climbed out through [[The Bronze Mouth]] into the
[[Inverted Pyramid]] and went home. The observatory is still unfound, and no
route down to it has been identified.

- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: Took the ladder down from the [[Great Hall]] through [[The Puddle]], the [[Astral Chamber]] and the [[Alchemy Hall]] to [[The Sealed Room]]. The observatory is still below.
- [[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]: The alchemy hall gave the first hard link between the Cradle and [[The Shadow]].
- [[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]]: Explored the last of the [[Great Hall]], found both cylinders join up, and left the Cradle through [[The Bronze Mouth]]. The observatory was never reached.
