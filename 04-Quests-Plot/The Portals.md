## Summary
The builders of [[The Cradle]] opened doors to other planes and kept them.

- The [[Poison Cage]] holds a portal to the para-elemental plane of poison,
  caged and still burning green when the party found it.
- The books in the [[Alchemy Hall]] record a way into the Astral Plane using the
  blood of [[The Astral Being]], which still hangs in its chains in the
  [[Astral Chamber]] with its blood dripping into flasks. The party's reading
  links this to the poison portal.

So the beast was not a specimen. It was a component.
