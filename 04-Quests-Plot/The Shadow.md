## Summary
The plague the party is trying to stop, and the thing the campaign is named for.

It is what happens when two diseases meet. Both are written up in the
[[Disease Books]] taken from the [[Alchemy Hall]]: one a hive mind, the other a
plague, with a handwritten note in the second about the unforeseen results of
the two coming into contact. Neither book gives a remedy for either disease,
though someone with more knowledge of the subject might be able to derive one.

Stopping the Shadow is why the party came to [[The Cradle]] in the first place
— see [[Into the Cradle]]. Session 06 is the first time the vault has a hard
link between the two: the facility built to study deadly things holds the
documentation for both halves of the plague.

Whether the builders made the Shadow, predicted it, or merely recorded it is not
yet established.
