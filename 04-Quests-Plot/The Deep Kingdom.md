## Summary
[[Zeernebooch]], an ambassador of the Deep Kingdom, made contact with the party
in the [[Glass Dome]]. The Deep Kingdom lies deeper than a normal person can
reach. According to Zeernebooch, the builders of this complex were studying the
ground and built a magical mechanism to bring things from the deep back up — and
the glass tubework appears to be a contraption built so the Deep Kingdom could
communicate with the surface at all.

Zeernebooch sealed the party in and announced it was hungry, so its stated
goodwill is in doubt. The nature of the mechanism, and what the builders were
raising with it, remain open.

## Session Log
- [[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]: First contact with Zeernebooch. Learned of the Deep Kingdom and the raising mechanism, then escaped after the ambassador sealed the dome.
- [[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]: The [[Crystal Sisters]] described [[The Cradle]] as a facility built to investigate deadly things, with an observatory at the bottom where the builders searched for materials — consistent with Zeernebooch's account of a mechanism to raise things from the deep.
