## Description
Three bottles of the white blood of [[The Astral Being]], taken from the
[[Astral Chamber]] after the party put the creature out of its misery.

This is the same fluid that had been dripping from it into the long-overflowed
flasks since before the party arrived. The books in the [[Alchemy Hall]] record
that the builders of [[The Cradle]] used it to open a portal into the Astral
Plane — see [[The Portals]]. Whether three bottles is enough for that, or
whether the party could work the method at all, is untested.

## Properties
- Nothing established in play. The portal use is what the builders' books
  claim, not something the party has tried.
