## Description
A clay construction found smashed on a table in the [[Astral Chamber]] and
repaired with *mending*.

Illustrated as a shallow dish about 25 cm across, hand-thrown from pale
grey-brown clay with a broad flat floor and a low rim. A fine graticule of
incised lines curves across the floor like a map projection, and N, E, S and
W are stamped into the rim at the compass points. Faint chalky white
spotting marks the rim where liquid dripped on it over a long time.

**Drawn seamless.** *Mending* restores an object as though it were never
broken, so no repair lines show; the note carries the story of finding it in
pieces. The dish form, the graticule and the compass letters were assigned
for illustration on 2 November 2026 — the note says only "a clay
construction".

## Properties
- Produces the plan of a building, or a perfect map of an area.
