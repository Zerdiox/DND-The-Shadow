## Description
A fist-sized wrinkly object resembling an old prune. Identified by
[[Echo of Twilight]] as dried brains — still slightly squishy.

Illustrated on bare chamber stone: a dark brown lump about 10 cm across,
deeply creased and folded, its skin leathery and dull, sagging slightly
under its own weight.

Drawn as what the party saw rather than as what Echo identified — the note
leads with the prune resemblance and the dried brains are his later reading,
so the plate follows that order. A first attempt describing it as a dried
organ came back with a generation error, which may have been a content
filter or may have been transient; the rewording was the more faithful
prompt either way. Assigned for illustration on 2 November 2026.

## Properties
- Do not spill blood on it: something may emerge. (Echo's warning — untested.)
