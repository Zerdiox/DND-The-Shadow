## Description
A finely crafted dagger with a hilt of thick leather.

Illustrated lying on a chest lid in the [[Armoury]]: about 30 cm overall,
with a steel disc pommel holding a deep red heart-shaped stone, a grip of
thick dark leather wrapped over the tang, a short straight crossguard, and a
bright double-edged blade through which fine garnet-red veins branch from
the guard towards the point.

The heart stone and the veined blade are both design reads of what the
dagger does — point it at a living creature and you feel its heartbeat —
rather than anything the table recorded. The note gives only the leather
hilt. Assigned for illustration on 2 November 2026.

## Properties
- +2 dagger.
- Pointing it at a living creature lets the wielder feel that creature's
  heartbeat, even through walls.
