## Description
A very pretty gemstone that gives off light of its own.

Illustrated on bare chamber stone: about 4 cm across, brilliantly clear and
sharply faceted, its facets filmed with fine dust, casting its own light
into a pool across the slab that picks out the cracks and pits around it.

**This is the one plate lit by its subject rather than by lamplight.** Every
other item in the set takes raking light from the upper left; here the stone
is the light source, so the frame falls into darkness beyond it. That
follows from the note rather than from taste — `magical: false`, and the
light is still recorded fact. Size and cut were assigned for illustration on
2 November 2026.

## Properties
- Dazzling to look at. A creature that sees it must succeed on a Wisdom check
  to look away.
- The pull strengthens the longer it holds a creature.
- Not magical — the effect is simply how dazzling it is.

## Session Log
- [[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]: Found by [[Echo of Twilight]] in chest 12 of the [[Vault]]. He failed a wisdom saving throw on opening it and became transfixed, and only broke free later — it grew harder to look away the longer it held him.
