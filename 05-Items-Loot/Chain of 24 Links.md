## Description
A chain of 24 links.

Illustrated lying in its chest: heavy dark iron links, each hand-forged as a
coiling tentacle gripping the next, with a larger ring of thick tentacles
woven into a closed circle partway along, and both loose ends finishing in
open curling tentacles.

The tentacles are a design read from what the chain does — it casts *Evard's
Black Tentacles* — in the same way the Throne Room's serpents were worked
into the Crown of Fear. Nothing in the note describes the chain's appearance
beyond its 24 links, and a plainly described iron chain came back looking
like builder's hardware. Assigned for illustration on 2 November 2026.

## Properties
- Casts *Evard's Black Tentacles*.
- Each casting consumes one link, so the chain holds 24 uses.
