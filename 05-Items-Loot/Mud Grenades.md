## Description
Four small bronze balls, each with a button on top.

Illustrated as one of the four, on bare chamber stone rather than in its
chest — small objects get a plain surface so they are not swamped. A cast
bronze sphere about 5 cm across with a fine seam round its circumference and
a recessed collar at the top. The size and the seam were assigned for
illustration on 2 November 2026.

The button came out as a decorative spiral boss rather than something that
looks pressed, which the user accepted rather than spend another
generation. The note's own wording is the authority: there is a button.

## Properties
- On exploding, turns stone within a 30-foot radius into mud.
