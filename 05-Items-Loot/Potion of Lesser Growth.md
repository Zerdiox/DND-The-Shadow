## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 1.

The liquid is the same amber as the [[Potion of Growth]] but visibly weaker,
thin and nearly colourless. The vials all carry the remains of a paper label,
faded past reading, which is why none of them could be told apart. Colour
assigned for illustration on 1 November 2026 and not recorded at the table.

## Properties
- As the [[Potion of Growth]], but smaller.
