## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 13.

A deep violet liquid in a thin-walled teardrop bulb, its mouth sealed over
with wax rather than stoppered — it is thrown, so it was never meant to be
opened. The liquid pools against the underside of the seal with empty air
below it, falling upwards. Assigned for illustration on 1 November 2026, not
recorded at the table.

## Properties
- Thrown, not drunk. Reverses gravity in a 20-foot radius for 6 rounds.
- Tingles on the tongue.
