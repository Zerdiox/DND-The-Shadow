## Description
Ten of the nineteen vials from the [[Alchemy Hall]] turned out to be poison:
vials 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18 and 19.

Carried by [[Echo of Twilight]] (7), [[Drivik]] (11), [[Aslaug]] (12, 14, 16),
[[Priest]] (15) and [[Muradin]] (18). Vials 5, 9 and 19 are unassigned.

The liquid is a murky dark green, cloudy rather than clear, in a tall
square-shouldered bottle. Colour assigned for illustration on 1 November 2026
and not recorded at the table — and note the illustration shows one bottle
and one green, while the notes say only that ten were poison. Whether they
held the same poison was never established.

## Properties
- Poison. The specific effects were not recorded.
