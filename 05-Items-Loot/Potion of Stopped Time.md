## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 3.

The liquid is colourless and utterly still, its surface flat as glass, in a
squat regular cylinder. A single bubble hangs motionless partway up, caught
mid-rise — a device for showing the effect, since a still liquid cannot
otherwise be told from an ordinary one in a picture. Colour and bubble
assigned for illustration on 1 November 2026, not recorded at the table.

## Properties
- Stops time for one full round.
- Rounds scale with the drinker's Intelligence modifier.
