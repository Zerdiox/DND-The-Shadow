## Description
A black coin that absorbs light — completely lightless to look at.

Illustrated on bare chamber stone: about 3 cm across, lying flat, its face a
flat void that gives back no highlight at all and reads as a hole cut into
the plate, with only a thin line of lamplight along the milled rim to show
it has thickness. A cast shadow keeps it an object rather than an actual
hole.

The milled rim and the 3 cm size were assigned for illustration on
2 November 2026; the note gives only that it is a black coin which absorbs
light.

## Properties
- Currency of lower and higher beings — demons, angels — used to buy and sell
  memories and other metaphysical things.
