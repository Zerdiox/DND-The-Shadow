## Description
Two books from the [[Alchemy Hall]]. One concerns a hive mind disease, the
other plagues. The plague book carries a handwritten note about the unforeseen
results of the two diseases coming into contact — see [[The Two Diseases]].

Between them they describe both halves of [[The Shadow]] — the plague the party
came to [[The Cradle]] to stop.

Neither contains a remedy, though someone with more knowledge of the subject
might work one out.

- Reference works. Not magical.
