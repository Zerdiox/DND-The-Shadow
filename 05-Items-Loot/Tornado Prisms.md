## Description
Two prisms, each with what appears to be a tornado spinning around inside it.

Illustrated as one of the pair, on bare chamber stone rather than in its
chest — small objects get a plain surface so they are not swamped. A clear
hexagonal crystal column with bevelled ends, the funnel standing in the
middle of the stone, wide at the top and tapering to a point that stops just
clear of the base. The hexagonal cut and the bevels were assigned for
illustration on 1 November 2026; the note records only "prisms".

Two known departures in that plate: it is lit from the upper right where the
rest of the set is lit from the upper left, and the prism is drawn
frame-filling like every other item rather than at its recorded thumb size.

## Properties
- Thumb-sized.
- Breaking one unleashes a tornado.
