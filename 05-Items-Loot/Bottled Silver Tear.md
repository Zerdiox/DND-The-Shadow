## Description
A bottle holding a single silver tear suspended in water. Known as a tear of
time.

Illustrated as a clear glass bottle about 12 cm tall, closed with a
ground-glass stopper seated in a chased silver collar, the tear a pale
silver-white liquid holding its shape motionless in the water with a faint
light about it. The vessel, the silver and the glow were assigned for
illustration on 1 November 2026 — the notes record only a silver tear
suspended in water, and nothing about it giving off light.

## Properties
- Drinking it undoes one specified event in the drinker's past.
- It always succeeds. The chance of terrible failure, as a percentage, equals
  the number of syllables in the request — so keep the wording short.
