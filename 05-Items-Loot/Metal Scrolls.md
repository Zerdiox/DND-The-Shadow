## Description
Four scrolls found in the [[Library]], made of a metal-like material that is
very sturdy and sharp. They cover two subjects, two scrolls each:

- *Addictions of Abyssal Gods*
- *Narcotics and Vices of the Creators*

They describe gods the party has never heard of — see [[Abyssal Gods]].

## Properties
- Not magical, and nothing to identify — these are documents, not magic
  items. The open questions are the text itself and where the metal came from.
