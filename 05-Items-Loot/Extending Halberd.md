## Description
A halberd head on a short handle that extends when activated. Masterwork.

Illustrated retracted, on a chest lid in the [[Armoury]]: a broad crescent
axe blade with a long top spike and a back spike, the head nearly as long as
the stubby haft beneath it, so the weapon looks top-heavy and plainly missing
most of its reach. A banded bronze collar partway down the haft marks where
it telescopes, and a plain iron butt cap closes the end.

The collar, the butt cap and the head's proportions were assigned for
illustration on 2 November 2026; the note gives only a halberd head on a
short extending handle. Being masterwork, it is drawn with only minimal wear
rather than the corrosion the other unidentified items carry.

## Properties
- Masterwork.
- The handle extends when activated.
