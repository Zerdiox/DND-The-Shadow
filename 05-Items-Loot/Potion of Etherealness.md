## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 4.

The liquid is a pale misty blue-white, half-transparent, in a round-bottomed
bulb that cannot stand on its own and rests in a small iron cradle, with a
long neck and a ground-glass stopper. It is the one vial in the set rendered
with any luminosity at all — the effect is half-presence, and a pale clear
liquid otherwise reads as water. Assigned for illustration on 1 November
2026, not recorded at the table.

## Properties
- Takes the drinker to the Ethereal Plane for 1 hour.
