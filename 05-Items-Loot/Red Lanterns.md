## Description
Lanterns fitted with a red lens.

Illustrated as one of them, unlit: a hand lantern about 30 cm tall with a
ring handle, a pierced iron smoke cap, four tall panes of deep red glass in
a black iron frame, and a flat base with a latched door. The four-paned
lantern form and the latched door were assigned for illustration on
1 November 2026; the note records only "a red lens". Nothing of the
black-and-white vision is shown, because that happens in the holder's eye
rather than in the lamp.

## Properties
- Whoever holds the lantern sees its light in black and white.
