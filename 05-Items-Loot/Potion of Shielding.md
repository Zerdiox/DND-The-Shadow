## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 6.

A cool steel blue-grey liquid in a wide squat hexagonal jar with a flat
ground-glass lid sealed at the rim. The surface does not lie flat: it domes
upward, the centre standing higher than where the liquid meets the glass, as
though held by something. Assigned for illustration on 1 November 2026, not
recorded at the table.

## Properties
- A shield bubble: +5 AC for 10 rounds.
