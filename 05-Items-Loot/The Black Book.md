## Description
A book with a black cover and silver lettering inside, wrenched from the dead
body of [[The Woman in the Ice]] — the thing her wraith stayed behind to guard.

## Properties
- Not magical.
- Concerns understanding people in any shape or form.
- So convoluted, and in such strange language, that [[Vicaster]] reckons months
  of study to make sense of it.
