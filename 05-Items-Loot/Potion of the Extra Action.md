## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 8.

A vivid amber-orange liquid in a double-gourd flask: two rounded chambers
stacked one above the other, divided by a pinched waist, each filled to the
same level so the same liquid shows twice over. The doubling is the vessel's
shape rather than any visible magic. Assigned for illustration on 1 November
2026, not recorded at the table.

## Properties
- Grants an extra action each round for 10 rounds.
