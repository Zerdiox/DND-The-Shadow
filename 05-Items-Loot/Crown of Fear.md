## Description
An ornamental crown recovered from the [[Throne Room]].

## Properties
- When a creature sees the wearer, that creature must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom
  saving throw or become frightened.
- The wearer is not affected.

## Session Log
- [[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]: Taken by [[Vicaster]], who put it on. The rest of the party rolled wisdom saves and were left in fear until he removed it. [[Drivik]]'s arcana check read enchantment magic on it.
