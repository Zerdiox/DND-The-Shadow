## Description
One of the nineteen unlabelled vials of coloured liquid from the cupboard in the [[Alchemy Hall]]. Vial 17.

A smoky translucent grey-green liquid in a flattened moon-flask with two
loop handles and a ground-glass stopper. The liquid's outline does not line
up with the flask's: a thin crescent of it stands clear of the glass on one
side, outside the vessel entirely. Assigned for illustration on 1 November
2026, not recorded at the table.

## Properties
- The drinker can travel through walls.
- Duration scales with the drinker's Intelligence modifier.
