## Description
A tongue of grey, silver-looking material, found under the oily water of
[[The Puddle]]. The only one of the three things in the puddle that showed up
under detect magic, as enchantment.

Observed, not identified: anyone who touches it grabs hold and immediately
starts tearing out their own tongue. *Dispel magic* stops it.
