## Summary
[[Vicaster]]'s god, and the patron behind his warlock pact.

Named in play for the first time in
[[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]], when Vicaster cast
*contact other plane* on the way back from [[The Cradle]] and asked who could
help with [[The Shadow]]. The answer was [[Orebridge]] — the party's own home
town — which led them to [[The Grove]].

See also [[Pantocrator]] and [[Abyssal Gods]]; no relation between them and
Aurorion has been established.
