## Summary
A kingdom lying deeper underground than a normal person can reach, known to the
party only through its ambassador [[Zeernebooch]]. Its people are said to have
difficulty communicating with the surface, and the glass tubework running through
the [[Glass Statue Room]] and [[Glass Dome]] appears to be a contraption built to
make that conversation possible at all.

Zeernebooch's account is the only source so far, and it sealed the party in and
declared itself hungry immediately afterwards — so treat all of it as unverified.

See [[The Deep Kingdom]] for the active plot thread.
