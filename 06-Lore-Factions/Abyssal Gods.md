## Summary
A set of gods named in the metal scrolls recovered from the [[Library]] —
*Addictions of Abyssal Gods* and *Narcotics and Vices of the Creators*. The
party has never heard of any of them. Their working theory is that these are
former pretenders.

The second title refers to "the creators", which may or may not mean the
builders of the [[Inverted Pyramid]] complex.
