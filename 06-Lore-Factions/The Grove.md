## Summary
A druid grove at [[Orebridge]]. The notes do not record a proper name for it —
the party refer to them simply as the druids.

They helped drive the invading dwarves out of [[Orebridge]] during the earlier
unrecorded stretch of the campaign, and the city's recovery is credited to
them. They have been working on the plague for a long time, which makes them
the people [[Aurorion]] meant: the lead on [[The Shadow]].

Asked for help, they said they need more time, and the party went on an
extended break.

They are **not** connected to [[Echo of Twilight]], who is a druid of the
Circle of the Stars.
