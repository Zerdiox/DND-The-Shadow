## Summary
Two of the books taken from the [[Alchemy Hall]] — the [[Disease Books]] —
each describe a disease the builders were working on: one a hive mind, the other
a plague.

The plague book carries a handwritten note about the unforeseen results of the
two coming into contact. The party knows what those results are: the two
diseases meeting is [[The Shadow]], the plague they are trying to stop.

Neither book gives a remedy for either disease, though someone with more
knowledge of the subject might be able to work one out. That makes these two
books the best lead the party has.
