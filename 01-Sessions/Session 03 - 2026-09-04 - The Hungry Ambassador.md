## Recap
The party entered the [[Throne Room]] and found an ornamental throne, mace and
crown beneath an unidentified serpent relief. [[Drivik]] read divination magic on
the throne, enchantment on the crown, and both on the mace. [[Muradin]] took the
[[Headhunter's Mace]] and [[Vicaster]] put on the [[Crown of Fear]], which drove
the rest of the party into fear until he removed it; [[Echo of Twilight]] then
sat on the throne and drew everyone's undivided attention.

Continuing on, they found the [[Glass Statue Room]], a network of glass tubes and
aquariums centred on a glass statue of an old wise man. Liquid flowed into the
statue, animating it, and it pointed the party towards a portal. Through it lay
the [[Glass Dome]], where the liquid revealed itself as [[Zeernebooch]], an
ambassador of the [[Deep Kingdom]]. It described a kingdom deeper than any person
can reach, and said the builders of the complex had been studying the ground and
made a magical mechanism to raise things from the deep. Then it sealed the door
and announced it was hungry. Spellcasting from Vicaster and a gorilla form from
Echo got the party out.

Beyond lay a [[Library]] of geology texts holding four [[Metal Scrolls]], and
above it a [[Vault]] of twenty large chests. The party worked through all twenty,
coming away with nineteen finds — gemstones, two separate currencies, and a spread
of curiosities none of them can identify. [[Echo of Twilight]] was caught staring
at the [[Radiant Gemstone]] and broke free only with effort, and the chest
[[Muradin]] had searched and called empty gave up the [[Vantablack Coin]] to
[[Vicaster]]. They then retreated to the main room with the deep hole.

## Key decisions
- Took the mace and crown from the throne room despite knowing both were magical.
- Followed the animated statue through the portal rather than avoiding it.
- Escaped the glass dome by force instead of negotiating with Zeernebooch.
- Opened all twenty chests in the vault rather than stopping partway.
- Retreated to the main room with the deep hole afterwards.

## Notable moments
- Vicaster wearing the Crown of Fear and frightening his own party.
- Echo of Twilight taking the throne and becoming the centre of everyone's attention.
- The glass statue filling with liquid and waving at Drivik.
- Zeernebooch sealing the dome and declaring itself hungry.
- Chest 1 reading as empty to Muradin, then yielding the vantablack coin to Vicaster.
- Echo pulling his eyes off the radiant gemstone as its hold kept tightening.
