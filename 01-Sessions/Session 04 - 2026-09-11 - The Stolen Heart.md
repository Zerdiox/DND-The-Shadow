## Recap
A short rest turned into an appraisal. The [[White Pearl]] glows under special
light and is worth about 400 gp; the [[Vantablack Coin]] is currency demons and
angels use to trade in memories, worth around 2,500; the [[Golden Beans]] are
the kidneys of a gold-eating mole at 10 gp apiece; and [[Echo of Twilight]] knew
the [[Wrinkled Prune]] for dried brains — best kept away from blood.

At [[The Shaft]], [[Muradin]] and [[Drivik]] together turned the handle and set
the chain moving, to no visible effect, and a lit stone dropped down the hole
never landed. The party took the stairs instead, down past the ore scales of the
[[Weighing Station]], and jumped the open shaft into the [[Cervit Storage]] —
Echo missed, and finished the jump as a raven. The crates held about 90 kg of
Cervit, an alloy nobody can make any more: [[White Cervit]] for fireproofing
armour and [[Black Cervit]], which turns aside *Ray of* spells. The party split it.

Above was the [[Orb Room]] and its four alchemical orbs of mutated plants; below,
the [[Fabric Room]] and then the [[Poison Cage]], a portal to the para-elemental
plane of poison that killed Vicaster's familiar and dealt him 22 damage. Beneath
that, in the [[Crystal Hall]], six [[Crystal Sisters]] were willing to talk. The
party returned the [[Milky Heart Crystal]] from the vault — their sister's stolen
heart — and the sisters told them where they were: [[The Cradle]], the goal of
their explorations. The whole complex beneath the [[Inverted Pyramid]] is the
Cradle, and an observatory waits at its very bottom.

Heading back, spectres wrapped in chains rose out of the fabric heaps; the party
beat them but they escaped. A ladder from the fabric room led to the
[[Strata Gallery]], where Echo read the labelled blocks as museum pieces from
worlds once ruled by the [[Pantocrator]].

## Key decisions
- Spent the short rest identifying the vault finds.
- Took the stairs beside the shaft rather than trying the chain.
- Split the Cervit across the party.
- Approached the Crystal Sisters politely and gave back the heart.
- Went back to search the fabric room.

## Notable moments
- Echo missing the jump and becoming a raven mid-fall.
- Drivik licking an alchemical orb, then a salt platform.
- Vicaster's familiar dying in the poison cage — and getting candy after.
- The Crystal Sisters allowing that the party might not be a treat.
- The strata labels: vampire strata, the Gackling moon, the burned city.
