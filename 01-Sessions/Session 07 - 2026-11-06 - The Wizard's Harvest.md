## Recap
The long rest over, the party climbed back up the ladders and stopped at
[[The Astral Being]] to see whether anything could be done for it. Nothing
could. [[Muradin]] threw a [[Mud Grenades|mud grenade]] to break the chains and
the creature came down, snapping off some of its spikes and landing in the mud
in exactly as much pain as before. [[Aslaug]] dug deep and said what everyone
already suspected: it was not made for this plane. Muradin took his warhammer to
it. It burst and deflated into a mess. The party left with three bottles of its
blood — [[Astral Blood]].

Then down the left-hand bronze cylinder of the [[Great Hall]]. The stair runs
through rock and out into an open cave, caged in metal the whole way, and black
butterflies drift there that look like holes punched in the air — the
[[Butterfly Cave]]. At the bottom is the [[Glass Floor Room]], whose floor
looks straight down into the [[Poison Cage]] they had walked through three
sessions ago. The stair at the far end goes back up and comes out in the *other*
bronze cylinder. Two ways down, one loop.

Out through the [[Armoury]] and up a long staircase to a small room and a long
hall. [[Drivik]] went ahead checking for traps, found none, and then looked up:
a shaft in the ceiling, thirty or forty feet, ending in a bronze mouth. A trap
door. [[Vicaster]] cast *fly* on Drivik, Muradin and himself — Muradin started
being sick the moment his feet left the floor, because dwarves don't do flying.
[[Echo of Twilight]] went up as an owl and Aslaug was carried. Drivik opened
[[The Bronze Mouth]] and they came out in the [[Inverted Pyramid]], among the
statues. Out of [[The Cradle]] at last, with the observatory never found.

Above ground, Vicaster cast *contact other plane* and spoke to [[Aurorion]].
The people who can help are in [[Orebridge]] — their own home town.

They walked back to [[The Dam]], and [[Rem Koolhuis]] was standing on top of it.
The wizard who ferried them there in the first place told them, unprompted, that
he is taking it: hold the dam, let the coming winter's melt power the three
golems already standing in front of it, and harvest magical power. Nobody drew a
weapon. The party went home.

Past [[Castle]], which is thriving, and into Orebridge, which has flourished —
trees in bloom, fields worked, the city recovered from the dwarven invasion with
[[The Grove]]'s help. They reported everything to [[Theodin]] and handed over the
books. He pointed them at the grove, who have been working on the plague for a
long time, and came along. The druids said they need more time. The party took
an extended break.

## Key decisions
- Killed [[The Astral Being]] rather than leave it hanging a second time.
- Took three bottles of its blood on the way out.
- Left [[The Cradle]] without finding the observatory.
- Did not fight [[Rem Koolhuis]], and did not agree to anything either.
- Handed the books over to [[Theodin]].

## Notable moments
- The mercy that wasn't: the grenade freed it, and it hurt just as much on the
  ground.
- Muradin airborne and immediately sick.
- Drivik being the only one to look up.
- Black butterflies like holes in the air.
- Standing on a glass floor over the poison portal they had once squeezed past.
- Rem Koolhuis on top of the dam, explaining his plan to the people best placed
  to stop him.
- Coming home and finding home doing fine.
