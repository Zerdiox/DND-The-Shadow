## Recap
Back up at the [[Strata Gallery]], [[Drivik]] jumped the shaft to the salt
chamber and caught something at the very edge of his darkvision. A second look
down showed it: something pale and white climbing the chain, filling the whole
shaft. [[Vicaster]]'s *eldritch blast* and [[Aslaug]]'s *heat metal* only
annoyed it, though repelling blast slowed it; *sickening radiance* did nothing
either. As it came up it resolved into a [[Shaft Giant]] compacted into the
space, hauling itself along with one free hand, its body a boneless liquid blob.

So the party went down instead, and spent the session trading levels with it.
Through the [[Crystal Hall]], where the [[Crystal Sisters]] were gone — home to
their own plane with their sister healed — and down to the [[Shell Room]] with its
empty clams and dried coral, across to the [[Art Store]] where the crates turned out to hold nothing
but art, down into the [[Floorboard Cave]], across again to the [[Tool Storage]]
and its unrecognisable implements. The giant gained twenty feet at a time.

Below that, the [[Spider Chamber]]: hieroglyphs on the walls and a spider-like
thing curled on its back, holding a block of ice with a woman inside. The giant
reached an arm down the ladder, grabbed Vicaster and lifted him to eat him,
until Aslaug's *command* made it drop him. Vicaster broke the ice. The wraith
that came out of it was the woman's own soul, and it screamed one thing before
the party put it down. They took [[The Black Book]] from her dead body — black
cover, silver lettering, months of study in strange convoluted language, and no
magic in it at all.

With the giant unable to follow and climbing back up, the party left through the
[[Stables]] and over [[The Bridge]], ninety feet across open darkness, into the
[[Great Hall]] — empty but for two bronze cylinders with stairs inside them.
Up the ladder and along a shelf in the cave wall was the [[Armoury]]: racks
stripped bare, ten locked iron chests still against the wall. Drivik picked
every one.

## Key decisions
- Chose to go around and below the giant rather than fight it.
- Left the art crated up in the [[Art Store]].
- Broke the ice block instead of leaving the body alone.
- Took the ladder up to the [[Armoury]] rather than the cylinders down.

## Notable moments
- The salt chamber standing empty. The sisters had gone home.
- Drivik catching the giant at 120 feet, right at the edge of his darkvision.
- Three spells in a row that only made it grumble.
- Vicaster lifted off the ladder to be eaten, and Aslaug's *command* dropping him.
- "STOP, THIS IS MY BOOK."
- Ten locked chests, ten picked locks, no traps.
