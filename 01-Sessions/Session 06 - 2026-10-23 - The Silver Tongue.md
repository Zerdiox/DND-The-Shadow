## Recap
Halfway down the ladder from the [[Great Hall]] a tunnel opened onto
[[The Puddle]] — oily, salty water with three things lying under it. Detect magic
lit up only one. [[Vicaster]] waded in and lifted the [[Green Sword]], which
caught fire the moment the blade cleared the water, then reached for the baby,
which screamed until the party's vision filled with black spots and [[Drivik]]
shoved it back under. Drivik hooked the [[Silver Tongue]] out on his rapier;
Vicaster shoved him into it, and Drivik began pulling out his own tongue until
[[Echo of Twilight]] dispelled it. They left the sword and the baby and took the
tongue, wrapped in canvas.

Further down, a square room where [[The Astral Being]] hangs from the ceiling in
chains, dripping white blood into flasks that overflowed long ago. [[Aslaug]]
knew it: a near-immortal thing of the Astral Plane, close to its end. Vicaster
mended a smashed clay construction on the table and found he had repaired the
[[Clay Mapmaker]].

Below that, the [[Alchemy Hall]]: dried-out equipment, cupboards of logbooks and
diaries, nineteen unlabelled vials — every one of them magical — and shelves of
project books with code names like *Angelic Pig*. Ten vials were poison. The
other nine were worth keeping. The books covered planar travel, military
fabrication, cursing cloth, poisons and diseases, and one of them explained the
thing upstairs: the builders had opened a way into the Astral Plane using its
blood. See [[The Portals]].

The two [[Disease Books]] mattered more. One covers a hive mind disease, the
other plagues, and the second carries a handwritten note about what happens when
the two meet. The party already knows the answer to that: it is [[The Shadow]],
the plague they came down here to stop. Neither book holds a remedy.

Then Drivik knocked on the great metal door at the far end, and something knocked
back. [[Muradin]] opened it. The room decompressed into the hall in a cloud of
metallic, acidic air, and [[The Enlarged Microbe]] came out of it. The fight was
hard. The long rest afterwards took everyone to level 9.

## Key decisions
- Left the burning sword and the screaming baby in the puddle; took the tongue.
- Left [[The Astral Being]] hanging in its chains.
- Took six books: the three on poison, the two on disease — both halves of [[The Shadow]] — one on planar travel and *Ways to Fly Easier*.
- Opened the metal door after something behind it answered the knock.

## Notable moments
- The sword igniting the instant it left the water.
- Drivik tearing at his own tongue, and Echo's dispel magic stopping him.
- Finding both halves of [[The Shadow]] written up on a shelf, with no remedy for either.
- Finding out the dying thing on the chains is how the builders reached the Astral Plane.
- Knocking on the door, and the door knocking back.
- Level 9.
