## Recap
The party exited the dam and encountered a giant bird bridge being eaten by eels. They pushed along the river, finding cracking earth, crustation corpses, and black tubes emitting green smoke. They reached a pier with a tower, crashed the pier, and explored the tower, revealing an inverted pyramid structure with Roman calcite statues, a giant wheel, and observation rooms containing prismatic keys and artifacts.

## Key decisions
- Healed the giant bird.
- Explored the tower and inverted pyramid.
- Decided not to touch the throne, crown, and mace.

## Notable moments

- Discovering the inverted pyramid structure.
