## Description
Echo of Twilight, tabaxi druid of the Circle of the Stars.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]**: Sat on the throne, drawing the undivided attention of the rest of the party. Used a gorilla form to help the party escape the sealing glass dome. Failed a wisdom saving throw on opening a chest in the vault and became transfixed by the [[Radiant Gemstone]] inside, breaking free only with effort. Also recovered the [[Flame Streamers]], [[Wrinkled Prune]], [[Restless Crystals]], [[Chain of 24 Links]] and [[Acidic Emeralds]].
- **[[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]**: Identified the [[Wrinkled Prune]] as dried brains during the short rest. Missed the jump across the shaft (5) and wild shaped into a raven to save himself. Read the plants in the [[Orb Room]] with nature (23), and recognised the [[Strata Gallery]] blocks as likely museum pieces from worlds ruled by the [[Pantocrator]].
- **[[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]**: Went down ahead of the giant with [[Aslaug]] and [[Vicaster]]. Identified the [[Chain of 24 Links]] as a casting of *Evard's Black Tentacles*, one link per use.
- **[[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]**: Broke the [[Silver Tongue]]'s hold on [[Drivik]] with *dispel magic*. Reached level 9 after the long rest.
- **[[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]]**: Turned into an owl to get up [[The Bronze Mouth]], while the rest of the party flew or were carried.
