## Description
An ambassador of the [[Deep Kingdom]]. Manifests as a liquid that flows through
the glass tubework of the [[Glass Statue Room]] and can fill and animate the
glass statue there, or spread through the walls of the [[Glass Dome]]. Speaks
through a contraption of narrowing chambers, apparently built because its people
have difficulty communicating with surface dwellers.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]**: Animated the glass statue and beckoned the party through the portal, then announced itself in the glass dome. Told the party about the Deep Kingdom and the complex's builders before sealing the dome's door and declaring itself hungry. The party escaped.
