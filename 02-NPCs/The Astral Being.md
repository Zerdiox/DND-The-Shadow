## Description
A crystalline structure with spikes, suspended from the ceiling of the
[[Astral Chamber]] on chains. It moves, and it is alive. [[Aslaug]] identified
it as a being of the Astral Plane, a kind normally close to immortal — but this
one is near its end.

The white liquid dripping from it into the long-overflowed flasks is its blood.
The books in the [[Alchemy Hall]] record that the builders used that blood to
open a portal into the Astral Plane. See [[The Portals]].

## Session Log
- **[[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]**: Found hanging in its chains and recognised by [[Aslaug]]. The party left it where it was.
- **[[Session 07 - 2026-11-06 - The Wizard's Harvest]]**: The party came back to try to help it and found they could not. [[Muradin]] threw a [[Mud Grenades|mud grenade]] to break the chains; it fell, snapping off some of its spikes, and lay in the mud in as much pain as before. [[Aslaug]] said plainly that it was not made for this plane. Muradin killed it with his warhammer. It burst and deflated. The party took three bottles of its blood — see [[Astral Blood]].
