## Description
A vast pale white thing climbing the chain in [[The Shaft]], filling the shaft
entirely. Compacted into the space, it drags itself upward with its one free
hand. Its body behaves like a liquid blob with no bones in it, and it can reach
an arm a long way down a ladder shaft after prey.

Known not to work on it: *eldritch blast*, *heat metal* and *sickening radiance*
all left it annoyed rather than hurt. The repelling effect of eldritch blast did
slow its climb, and *command* made it release a grabbed creature.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]**: Spotted by [[Drivik]] at the edge of his 120 ft darkvision, crawling up the chain. The party spent the session outrunning it downward, trading levels with it. It grabbed [[Vicaster]] through the [[Spider Chamber]] ladder and tried to eat him until [[Aslaug]]'s *command* made it drop him. Unable to follow further down, it turned and began climbing back up.
