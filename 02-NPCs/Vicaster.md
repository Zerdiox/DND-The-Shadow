## Description
Vicaster Cloudwink, eladrin warlock of the Celestial patron. The party member
whose perspective the raw session notes are written from.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 03 - 2026-09-04 - The Hungry Ambassador]]**: Picked up the Crown of Fear and placed it on his head, forcing the rest of the party into a fear effect until he removed it. Sent his familiar ahead to make first contact with [[Zeernebooch]], and helped the party escape the sealing glass dome with clever spellcasting. Opened five of the vault's chests, recovering the [[Fire Crystals]], [[Mavemaren Bank Notes]], [[Smoke Fabric]], [[Tornado Prisms]] and the [[Loathsome Garnet]] — then found the [[Vantablack Coin]] in chest 1, which [[Muradin]] had searched and called empty.
- **[[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]**: Dropped a lit stone down [[The Shaft]]; it fell out of sight. Scouted ahead with his familiar all session — in sphinx of wonder form it died passing into the [[Poison Cage]], and he resummoned it and gave it candy. Took 22 poison damage near the portal and identified it as leading to the para-elemental plane of poison. Led the greeting of the [[Crystal Sisters]] with a bow.
- **[[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]**: Opened on the [[Shaft Giant]] with *eldritch blast*, which annoyed it more than it hurt, though repelling blast slowed its climb; *sickening radiance* did no better. Held it off while the others climbed down. It then reached an arm down and seized him, and nearly ate him. He broke the ice block in the [[Spider Chamber]], judged its cold unlike the library wraiths at home, and made the first reading of [[The Black Book]]. Also identified the [[Tornado Prisms]].
- **[[Session 06 - 2026-10-23 - The Silver Tongue]]**: Cast detect magic on the puddle, waded in and lifted out the [[Green Sword]], which burst into flame, then the baby, which screamed. Shoved [[Drivik]] into the [[Silver Tongue]] and later carried it wrapped in canvas. Mended the smashed clay construction into the [[Clay Mapmaker]], read the alchemy hall's library, and took six books. Reached level 9 after the long rest.
