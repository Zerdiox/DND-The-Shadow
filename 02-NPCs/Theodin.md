## Description
The leader of [[Orebridge]]. The party reported to him on their return from
[[The Cradle]] and lent him the [[Disease Books]] and the [[Poison Books]] they
had taken from the [[Alchemy Hall]]. He pointed them at [[The Grove]], who have
been working on the plague for a long time, and went with them.

The books are the only written account of both halves of [[The Shadow]], and
they are out of the party's hands while the grove works.
