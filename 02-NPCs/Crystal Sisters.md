## Description
Six female figures made of crystal, with spikes growing from their bodies and
eyes of broken gemstones. They lounge on salt platforms and speak one at a time,
each in turn. They were brought to [[The Cradle]] against their will from a place
where drought and purification rule. Time is relative to them; they stayed
because one of the sisters had her heart stolen.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 04 - 2026-09-11 - The Stolen Heart]]**: Amused by [[Vicaster]]'s familiar, they allowed that the party might not be a treat if it was polite. Accepted the [[Milky Heart Crystal]] — their sister's stolen heart — and healed her with it. Told the party they are in the Cradle, a facility built to investigate deadly things, with an observatory at the very bottom as its central point.
- **[[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]**: Gone. The party passed back through the [[Crystal Hall]] and found it empty — with their sister's heart returned and she herself healed, the sisters had gone home to their own plane.
