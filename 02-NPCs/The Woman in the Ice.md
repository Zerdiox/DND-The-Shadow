## Description
A human woman whose body was held in a block of ice in the legs of the
spider-like construct in the [[Spider Chamber]]. Her soul remained as a wraith,
bound to the ice and to [[The Black Book]] she was buried with.

[[Vicaster]] found the cold on the block reminiscent of the cold wraiths the
party met at home in the library, but judged that the magic came from neither
the body nor from contained wraith magic. Its source is unexplained.

## Session Log
- **[[Session 05 - 2026-10-16 - This Is My Book]]**: Vicaster broke the ice block. Her wraith burst out screaming "STOP, THIS IS MY BOOK," and the party beat it. They wrenched [[The Black Book]] from her dead body.
